## Observation (not a defect of this change)

`GET /instances/:id/attachments/:attachment_id` on a missing attachment appends an `attachment.access_denied` audit entry
(REQ-388, by design) for any caller who holds `:AttachmentsRead`, the auditor included. The exhaustive sweep therefore tolerates
exactly that one append-only audit action and asserts no other table changes.
