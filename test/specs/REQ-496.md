## Isolation

Every DB test provisions its own tenant(s) through `TenantFixture.provisioned_tenant!/1` and is
`async: false` (sandbox `:auto`, DDL, real transactions). Global rows written by pack installs
(`solution_pack_installs`, `solution_pack_artefact_bases`, `entity_column_promotions`,
`pack_update_resolutions`) are deleted in an `on_exit` registered after the tenant fixture's. No
test depends on wall-clock time or unseeded randomness; ids are unique per run; there are no
sleeps and no cross-connection lock waits.
