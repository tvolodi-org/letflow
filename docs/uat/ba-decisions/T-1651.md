   The pilot plan (line 32) still says imported users' sign-in "depends on REQ-788/789 (task T-1632, in review)", which is now both stale and misleading.
3. **REQ-800 missed that counts already have people.** A count order carries `chair_employee_id` and `member_employee_ids`
   (`@input_keys` in `lib/letflow/modules/stock/counts.ex`; used by `count_print.ex`, `count_paperwork.ex`, `count_differences.ex`). REQ-800 grepped
   `assignee|counter_id|counted_by|assigned_to` and found nothing, then asked "do counts get an owner at all". They do: a chair and members, who are
   employees (`org_employee`, with an optional `user_id` link, `lib/letflow/modules/org/org.ex` moduledoc and `import.ex` field list).
