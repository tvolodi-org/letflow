# BA decision for UAT: what the Meridian test accounts sophie, lars and claudia may hold (GH #2935)

Business analyst: meridian (UAT).

## Question

On QA, `actor-meridian-sophie`, `actor-meridian-lars` and `actor-meridian-claudia` carry PROCESS_OPERATOR as well as TASK_WORKER. The roster (`test/fixtures/uat/actors.yaml`) lists TASK_WORKER only for all three. Which is intended: (a) TASK_WORKER only, strip PROCESS_OPERATOR from the QA realm and the seed; or (b) PROCESS_OPERATOR is intended, update the roster and re-check the refusal scenarios that use these actors as the wrong actor?

## Decision (UAT only)

1. **Lars: TASK_WORKER only (option a).** Lars is the independent L1 approver. No Meridian scenario needs him to start an instance, see the whole queue or reassign tasks. PROCESS_OPERATOR on his account has no business reason, and it lets him act as an operator where he is meant to be a plain approver. Strip it from the QA realm; the roster stays as it is.
2. **Sophie and claudia: PROCESS_OPERATOR is intended, as the initiators (option b), and the roster must say so.** Sophie (analyst) starts every loan application and claudia (compliance officer) starts every regulatory review. TASK_WORKER cannot start an instance (`InstancesStart`: no), so without PROCESS_OPERATOR step 1 of those scenarios is refused. PROCESS_OPERATOR is the least built-in role with that permission (same reasoning as T-0321). The roster entries for sophie and claudia are changed to `[TASK_WORKER, PROCESS_OPERATOR]` with a note that the second role is there to start the process.
3. **The rule for UAT test accounts in general:** an account holds a built-in role beyond TASK_WORKER only if a scenario step it performs needs that role's permission; the roster lists every such role and the reason; an account never holds a role "because the realm has it". A persona used as the wrong person in a refusal scenario must not hold a wider role than the scenario's text assumes.
4. **Consequence for the refusal scenarios:** sophie and claudia are the initiators, not the "nearest wrong person" for the approvals and the disbursement, but an operator sees every task and may reassign. A refusal that targets them (approve, release money, change users) is therefore valid only for the reason the scenario states (no routing role, no approval right), not because the account is narrow. Where a refusal needs a plain account, the scenario should use a different persona that holds TASK_WORKER only (for example `actor-meridian-oliver`, no routing role) as the wrong actor. This re-check is a separate UAT-design task; I did not edit scenarios here.
5. **Seed and realm:** the seed header says "TASK_WORKER implicit for every persona". To match, the seed (or the realm export) must record PROCESS_OPERATOR for sophie and claudia explicitly, and lars must lose it. That is a change to the QA realm and seed, not a BA edit; it is listed for the team to file.
