# BA decision for UAT: where the org unit, responsible person and location of an asset come from (task T-1486)

Business analyst: assets (UAT). Question of the owner, 2026-11-08, from the gate C walk on gate-b5 (build 00643fd7).
The issue number was not known to the analyst when writing; the file is named after the task, as `T-0321.md` is.

## Question

Today the asset card has a free "Org unit" picker, no location, responsible person or user (those live only in the
balance rows), and cost and depreciation typed by hand. The owner suspects the card's org unit, responsible person and
location must not be typed freely but come from the accounting side. The project manager's reading to confirm or correct:
documents are the source (commissioning, change of holder, hand over, write off, change of kind); the registers are the
result of posting documents; the card shows the current state taken from the last posted document; initial values enter
through a commissioning or an opening-balance document. Four parts: (1) is that the right model; (2) which card fields
stay editable and which become read-only or derived; (3) how an existing asset enters the books; (4) the gap tasks with sizes.

## Decision (short)

1. **Yes, the model is right.** Documents are the source, the registers are their result, the card shows the current
   state. This is what the written decisions already say; what is missing is the card following it.
2. **Descriptive fields stay editable; holder fields are derived and read-only.** Org unit, location, responsible person,
   user and quantity are never typed on the card after the asset exists; they are shown from the register. Book value stays
   an information field.
3. **Existing assets enter through a dedicated asset opening document**, not through a commissioning with an old date. A
   commissioning with an old date is impossible by the product's own rule (below).
4. Six gap tasks, listed in section "Gap tasks".

## Reasons

### 1. The model

Sources opened:

* The owner decision register, `/home/tvolodi/dev/ai-dala/ai-dala-portfolio/DECISIONS.md` (a sibling checkout on the
  same host, **outside this worktree**; opened by the analyst on 2026-11-08 and quoted here so the quote can be checked
  without it). D-061 (line 65): "two read-only registers per module (balances, movements), corrections by new entries";
  "depreciation is an info field (imported), not calculated". D-097 (line 139, item 3): "inventory_number is unique per
  tenant; one currency per tenant (KZT default, field kept); old assets arrive by the xlsx import (C17/C18)". The in-repo
  files `docs/assets/asset-workflow-documents.md` (lines 8-11) and `docs/assets/asset-register.md` (line 70) restate
  D-061 and D-097 in the same words, so the repository alone carries these rules. The sentence "only workflows write to the
  registers" is the owner's shorthand in the question and in asset-workflow-documents.md line 8; it is not a verbatim
  line of D-061.
* D-097 item 3 also settles part of question 3: old assets arrive by the xlsx import. It names the channel, not the
  document; the opening document below is the document the import fills (UAT assumption).
* `docs/assets/asset-register.md` section 3 (last paragraph): "The card has no location, materially responsible person,
  actual user or quantity field. Those values live only in the register, so the card and the register cannot disagree."
  Section 7.1: "The quantity of an asset is the sum of its balance rows ... not a card field." Section 7.2, last item: for a
  fixed or intangible asset the card's `org_unit_id` equals the `org_unit` slot of the balance row with quantity 1, "kept
  equal by the documents of item C2 in the same transaction as the posting".
* `docs/assets/asset-workflow-documents.md` sections 2 to 5 (commission, change, hand over, write off: each one posting;
  the register legs carry org unit, location, responsible, user) and the note in section 1.4: "The card `org_unit_id` is not
  yet updated by a posted hand over (follow-up part 2)."

So the written design already is the PM's reading: the register is the result of postings, and the org unit of the card is
meant to follow the postings. Two things in the shipped state contradict it:

* the card's `org_unit_id` is a required, freely editable field (asset-register.md section 3, row `org_unit_id`: "the org
  unit that holds the card"), so a user can make the card say one unit while the register says another;
* a posted hand over does not yet move the card (section 1.4 note).

The first is the real gap. It is also a safety gap, because the card's org unit is the access scope of the card (section
7.2, decision 0076 R7): a free picker lets a user move a card into or out of a unit's visibility without any document.

Kazakhstan practice. This is general accounting knowledge, **not a source in this repository**, so it is marked as such:
**UAT assumption, to be confirmed by the process owner.** In Kazakhstan, as in the CIS practice the printed forms come from,
a fixed asset is put on the books by a document (act of acceptance and commissioning), moved by a document (internal
movement act), written off by a document (write-off act), and the inventory card of the asset is a record kept from those
documents. The repository confirms the form family exists (`docs/assets/kz-forms-mapping.md` rows 23 and 24: the card of
leased fixed assets, the act of write-off of vehicles) but does not set out the rule, so the accountant of the process owner
confirms it. Nothing in the answer depends on it: D-061 alone settles the model.

### 2. Which card fields stay editable

The rule: **a field that describes the asset stays editable; a field that says who holds it, where, and how many is derived.**
Proposal for UAT, to be confirmed by the owner at the gate of phase C.

| Field | Before the first posting | After the first posting | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| inventory_number | editable | read-only | it is the identity used by documents, xlsx import and printed forms; D-097 makes it unique per tenant. Changing it after a posting would change what printed documents say. UAT assumption |
| name | editable | editable (audited) | descriptive; asset-register.md section 6 says a field change is an audited card update, not a posting |
| kind | editable | read-only | already decided: "The kind of a card does not change once the card has a posting" (asset-register.md section 3); a change of kind has its own document (docs/assets/change-of-kind.md) |
| org_unit_id | not typed; set by the document that creates the card | read-only, equals the register | derived (see below) |
| location, responsible, user, quantity | not on the card | shown on the card from the register (card panel, REQ-757) | already the design; the card panel shows them |
| acquisition_date, currency, acquisition_cost | editable | editable (audited) | information only; D-061 and asset-register.md section 6: not calculated, typed or imported |
| accumulated_depreciation, book_value, depreciation_as_of | editable | editable (audited) | information only; not calculated in the first version |
| valid_until | editable (intangible only) | editable | descriptive |
| active | editable | editable; refused if the register still holds a balance | retires the card; no delete. The refusal is UAT assumption |
| external_ref_system, external_ref_id | editable | editable | link to an outside system |

How the org unit reaches the card: the card is created by a document (a commission or opening line creates it, as the
change of kind already creates cards, change-of-kind.md section 3 and 7). Its org unit is then **the org unit of the last
posted document that put the asset there**, written in the same transaction as that posting (asset-register.md section 7.2
last item). A hand over to another org unit moves the card; a change of responsible or user does not, because the org unit
stays. A reversal restores it. For a low-value asset whose quantity is split over several org units, the card has no single
org unit; this is the open question 3 of asset-register.md section 8 and stays open (the owner has not answered it).

Until the first card exists no document can name it, so a card created by hand (the "new asset" button today) has to be
created together with its first document, or by import; section "Gap tasks", G1.

### 3. How an existing asset enters the books

**Recommendation: a dedicated asset opening document**, parallel to the stock one. Reasons:

* A commissioning posting takes its event time from the document and is refused when older than 96 hours
  (`event_time_too_old`); 96 is the default, 720 hours is the registration cap, and no tenant or operator override exists
  (decision 0073 D-T, restated in `docs/migration/decisions/0079-opening-balances-and-ledger-xlsx.md` D1, and in
  asset-workflow-documents.md section 1.1). So a commissioning with an old acquisition date cannot be posted. Taking the
  other road, a commissioning dated today with the real date in a field, would make a 2019 asset look commissioned today in
  the movements report and would mix real purchases with cut-over entries.
* The stock side already did this: `docs/assets/stock-workflow-documents.md` section 4a (REQ-725, REQ-726) and decision
  0079-opening-balances D1 to D6: the entry's event time is its posting time, the real date is a field, two people post it
  (submit, approve with `distinct_from`), a wrong opening is reversed as a whole and redone (D6).
* `docs/assets/asset-xlsx.md` section 8, question 1, already names the alternative ("an asset opening document reusing
  decision 0079 D1 to D6") and proposes, as a UAT assumption, commission documents typed one by one. That proposal fails
  for old assets for the reason above and for volume (an enterprise has thousands of old assets), so it is superseded here.

Shape of the document (proposal, UAT assumption, to be confirmed at the gate of phase C): a header with org unit, note and
an optional external reference pair; lines with inventory number, name, kind, location (empty for intangible),
responsible person, actual user (optional), quantity, the real acquisition date, and the information values (cost,
accumulated depreciation, book value, as-of date). The posting creates the card and posts cause `opening` (one positive leg
per line) to the asset ledger; no backdating. An import by xlsx fills the lines, 200 rows per file (decision 0077 D2; a UAT
assumption there too). The "no price on documents" rule of asset-register.md section 6 and assets.md review finding 4 is
kept in this way: the information values are carried **to the card** as the card is created, they are not a ledger slot or
leg column, and they are not calculated. Whether an opening line may carry them at all is the one place this decision
touches that rule, so it is listed as an assumption.

Commissioning stays the way to bring in a **new** asset (bought, received) with a date within 96 hours.

### 4. Does this fit D-061 ("only workflows write to the registers")?

Yes, and it closes a hole. With the changes above, the only ways to move an asset are documents (commission, opening, change,
hand over, write off, change of kind), each posted by a workflow. The card's holder fields stop being a second, human-typed
source for the same facts, so card and register cannot disagree.
