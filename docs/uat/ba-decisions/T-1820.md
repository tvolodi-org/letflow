# BA decision: granular fertilizer batches with bagging, three options (task T-1820, MES-3)

Business analyst. Owner request 2026-11-12. No issue number was known; the file is named after the task, as `T-1818.md` is. DRAFT design only: no code, no client contact, nothing on production, money, DNS, secrets, demo or a public repository.

## Question

How would batch production of several granular fertilizer types, packed in bags of different sizes, be modelled on Letflow (stock documents, lots, assets, process designer)? Cover recipe/BOM with versions, batch order, consumption by lot, yield and losses, bagging output by bag size and pallet, finished goods lots, QC sample and release, recall both ways. Give three options with effort and risk.

## Decision (short)

Full text: `docs/design/mes/03-granular-batch-options.md` (options, comparison table, traceability design, 14 client questions).

| Option | Idea | Effort (assumption) | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Existing items, lots, stock documents and one process definition; batch by convention | 1 to 3 weeks | A held lot is not blocked from shipment; trace is manual; yield is manual |
| B | New batch-record module (recipe versions, batch, consume/produce tied to batch, lot quality status, QC, packaging and pallets, trace) on top of stock | 8 to 14 weeks | Touches the shared ledger policy (new causes, hold rule) on a tenant-data path |
| C | B plus equipment states, downtime/OEE, shift logs, machine data | 20 to 35 weeks | Size, no real plant data, machine feed security |

Recommendation (a proposal, not an owner decision): plan the build as **B**; use **A** only as a learning pilot; treat **C** as phase 2 after a client uses B.

## Reasons

* The ledger key already has the lot (`lib/letflow/modules/stock/ledger_policy/declare.ex`), so genealogy can be built on it. Its causes are a closed list with no consume/produce, so B must change the ledger policy.
* Lots have no quality status (`lib/letflow/modules/catalog_items/lots.ex`), so "hold" cannot be enforced without new work; that is the key difference between A and B.
* Equipment exists only as asset cards (`assets/assets.ex`) and locations; states, downtime and OEE do not exist (part 1, rows 6 and 11), which is what separates C from B.
* B closes the largest gaps of part 1 for batch plants (rows 2, 3, 4, 8, 13).
