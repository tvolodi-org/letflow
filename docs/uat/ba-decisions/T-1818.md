# BA decision: MES domain note and fit/gap (task T-1818, MES-1)

Business analyst. Owner request 2026-11-12. No issue number was known; the file is named after the task, as `T-1814.md` is. DRAFT design only: no code, no client contact, nothing on production, money, DNS, secrets, demo or a public repository.

## Question

In plain words, what does a MES do for process (liquid), batch (granular plus bagging) and discrete (electronics) plants, and how much of it exists on Letflow today?

## Decision (short)

Letflow already covers the **base layer** of a MES: item and lot masters, a stock ledger with the lot in its key, locations, codes and labels, assets (usable as equipment), versioned documents and print, a hash-chained audit, a process engine with a four-eyes rule, modules, roles, mobile offline. It has **no production layer**: no recipe or BOM, no work order or batch, no consume/produce booking, no quality release, no downtime or OEE, no e-signature, no serial-level station tracking. The full table (18 functions, with needed-by, where it exists, gap, size) is in `docs/design/mes/01-domain-and-fit-gap.md`.

Recommended order for the next design parts (a proposal only): recipes and BOM, work orders and batches, consume/produce on the ledger, quality release, then batch record and signatures. Discrete serial tracking waits until the owner clarifies the electronics prospect.

## Reasons

* Every "exists" claim was read in the code on this branch (list in the design file, section 4). Searches for oee, downtime, recipe, formula, bill of material and work order in `lib` found no production module; `priv/modules` holds only assets, catalog_items, codes, exam, locations, org, stock.
* The stock ledger key already contains the lot (`lib/letflow/modules/stock/ledger_policy/declare.ex`), so genealogy can be built on it; its seven causes are a closed list, so consume/produce changes the ledger policy (marked in the table).
* `validate_capture/3` in `catalog_items.ex` states in its moduledoc that nothing calls it yet; lot capture is therefore not enforced today.

## UAT assumptions, to be confirmed by the process owner

* ISA-95, ISA-88 and GMP summaries are from general knowledge. The standards and public pages were **not opened** in this task; they must be checked before the note is shared.
* Size scale S/M/L is a rough comparison with existing modules, not a measurement.
* "GMP-like" means: recorded steps, no silent change, who/when/why, second-person release. No legal validation (21 CFR Part 11, EU Annex 11) is claimed.
* The lot for a liquid stream (tank fill, shift or time window) is an open owner question, not decided.
