# BA decision for UAT: may a SwiftRoute UAT account sit in a tenant-local PLATFORM_ADMIN group? (GH #2939)

Business analyst: platform UAT (scenario `platform-refusal-customer-administrator-boundaries`).

## Question

On QA, `actor-swiftroute-alice` carries only TASK_WORKER and TENANT_ADMIN in her token, but she is a member of SwiftRoute's tenant-local PLATFORM_ADMIN group. The scenario precondition says the SwiftRoute administrator holds the administrator role of SwiftRoute only, not any platform operator role. Is the membership intended? The issue offers: (a) remove the membership on QA; (b) keep it as a deliberate negative test and record it; (c) ask whether a tenant may have a PLATFORM_ADMIN group at all.

## Facts checked

- `docs/roles.md`: "A tenant-side actor holds TENANT_ADMIN or a tenant role, never PLATFORM_ADMIN." PLATFORM_ADMIN is "honoured only in the platform tenant; not seeded or issuable anywhere else"; its holder "must not act inside a customer tenant or read a customer's business data".
- `docs/migration/decisions/0046-admin-scopes-and-role-charter.md` D4: PLATFORM_ADMIN "is not seeded, not issuable and not honoured in any other tenant". D2: the operator role is honoured only in the config-pinned platform tenant.
- `test/fixtures/uat/actors.yaml`: the roster lists alice as `[TASK_WORKER, TENANT_ADMIN]`, with the note "no PLATFORM_ADMIN" (infra T-0177 re-check 2026-10-07). `legacy_platform_admin: {}` states that no tenant actor holds PLATFORM_ADMIN.
- `test/fixtures/uat/scenarios/platform/refusal-customer-administrator-boundaries.yaml` precondition (line 30): the SwiftRoute administrator holds the administrator role of SwiftRoute only, not any platform operator role.
- Issue #2939 (read with its comments; there are none besides the body): all of alice's platform-scope requests were refused, so the refusal scenario passed and this is not a product defect.

I did not query QA myself; the membership is as reported in the issue.

## Decision (UAT only)

**Option (a).** The membership is not intended in UAT.

1. The UAT environment must match the roster: alice has no PLATFORM_ADMIN, directly or through a tenant-local group. The precondition of the scenario stays as written and is not reworded.
2. The infra seed (`qa-uat-env.sh`) should remove her from SwiftRoute's PLATFORM_ADMIN group. That is an infra change outside this team's repository; it needs the infra owner and is not done here.
3. Until it is removed, a UAT run that uses this actor reports the precondition as NOT MET (BLOCKED for the precondition, with the refusal results noted as observed). It is not a silent PASS: the refusals are real evidence, but they were obtained under a precondition that did not hold literally.
4. Option (b) is not adopted for this account. A deliberate "tenant-local PLATFORM_ADMIN group grants nothing" check is a good negative test, but it needs its own named actor and its own scenario, so that the administrator-boundary scenario keeps a clean precondition. If wanted, it is a separate scenario request; no actor is invented here.

## Reasons

- The charter and 0046 D4 say a tenant actor never holds PLATFORM_ADMIN and that it is not issuable outside the platform tenant. A membership contradicts the roster, so the environment, not the roster, is wrong.
- Hiding a membership behind a passing refusal weakens the scenario: "no platform operator role" is the claim being tested, and a reader of the report would assume it was literally true.
- Removing a membership that grants nothing in this build costs nothing and removes the doubt.

## Not decided here (production / owner)

- Option (c): whether a tenant may have a group named PLATFORM_ADMIN at all, or whether the product should refuse creating one, touches production behaviour and access. UAT assumption: none made. It is for the process owner / product owner and, as 0046 D4 says "not issuable", may be a product defect worth a separate issue (reported in findings; this team does not file it).
- The related QA-accounts decision of #2931/#2932 is not needed for this ruling.

Decision for UAT only; the process owner configures production.
