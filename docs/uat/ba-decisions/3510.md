# BA decision for UAT: should the SwiftRoute CEO persona keep TENANT_ADMIN (issue #3510)

Business analyst: swiftroute (UAT).

## Question

`actor-swiftroute-alice` (CEO) holds TASK_WORKER and TENANT_ADMIN in the QA realm swiftroute. She does ordinary
business steps (CEO co-signature, CEO rejection, escalated approval, incident escalation target). Does the admin
role make her pass hollow or hide separation of duties?

Option (a): keep it and record that the CEO is also the tenant administrator in this vertical.
Option (b): remove TENANT_ADMIN from her and add a separate `actor-swiftroute-admin` persona (a request to the infra owner).

## Decision (UAT only)

**Option (a): keep TENANT_ADMIN on `actor-swiftroute-alice` and record it**, under these limits:

1. In this vertical the CEO is also the tenant administrator. This is a stated fact of the UAT environment, not a hidden one. The roster note for her already records it.
2. A scenario that claims separation of duties (nobody can approve past the process, or grant themselves a responsibility) must not use Alice as the account that is refused. Those refusal steps use personas without admin rights (the dispatcher and the Operations Manager, as `refusal-user-administration` already does).
3. Alice's business steps are judged as a CEO: they must hold because of her CEO responsibility. The run report names the responsibility the task was routed to, so a reader can see the step did not pass on admin rights alone.
4. This decision widens BA decision #3221 limit 1, which says: "It is used for the advance-timer action and for nothing else in a scenario. It never performs a persona's business step and never serves as the actor of a refusal check." The tooling account `swiftroute-admin-user` may now also do read-only user-administration read-backs that need the user-management permission, and nothing else: never a business step, never the actor of a refusal check, and the run report names the account for each read-back. Reason: Alice is not the default account for those reads, so the read-backs do not rest on a business persona. #3221 limits 2-4 stand unchanged.
5. No scenario claims "the CEO cannot administer users" or "the CEO cannot reassign tasks". Such a claim would be false for this environment.
6. No infra request is made now. If the owner later wants the CEO without admin rights, option (b) stays open. The request to the infrastructure owner would then be: "In the QA realm swiftroute, remove realm role TENANT_ADMIN from actor-swiftroute-alice and create actor-swiftroute-admin (TENANT_ADMIN only); update qa-uat-env.sh to match." The BA would then update the roster, the first-administrator scenarios and `refusal-user-administration`. This task changes no seed, realm or definition.

## Reasons

- **Admin rights do not replace the CEO responsibility.** `docs/roles.md` says a principal holding a task route permission tenant-wide is exempt only from the TASK_WORKER grant requirement; "the routing-role checks stay in force for the exempt principal". Alice can complete the CEO's tasks only because she is in the CEO routing role, so her pass is not owed to TENANT_ADMIN. Limit 3 lets the report show this.
- **The first-administrator scenarios depend on her.** `swiftroute/tenant-onboarding-happy` names her as the tenant administrator (alias `tenant_admin`), and `swiftroute/first-login-administrator-binding` uses her as the pending administrator. In `docs/roles.md` the first administrator is named by e-mail at onboarding and becomes TENANT_ADMIN at first login. Removing the role would break the onboarding story of this vertical, or force a new administrator persona and rewrites of both scenarios, for no gain in what the CEO scenarios prove.
- **The separation-of-duties claims are carried by other accounts.** `refusal-user-administration` already tests that the dispatcher and Operations Manager cannot change users or roles, so the claim the issue worries about is checked with non-admin personas. The CEO scenarios do not claim it.
- **Option (b) is an access change in the QA realm** (create an account, remove a role), which belongs to the infra owner and is not needed to keep the results honest.
- **A small business, a combined role is realistic.** A company the size of the SwiftRoute persona has its CEO as the administrator. UAT reflects that, and the risk is stated instead of hidden.
