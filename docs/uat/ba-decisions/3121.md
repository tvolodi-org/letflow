# BA decision for UAT: roster role of Vortex anna, nina and karl (PROCESS_OPERATOR vs TASK_WORKER) (GH #3121)

Business analyst: Vortex UAT. This file changes no code, seed, definition, test, fixture or QA account. What follows for each is stated below and applied by later tasks.

## Question

QA gives `actor-vortex-anna`, `actor-vortex-nina` and `actor-vortex-karl` the roles TASK_WORKER and PROCESS_OPERATOR. The roster `test/fixtures/uat/actors.yaml` (anna and nina lines 120-129, karl lines 115-119) lists TASK_WORKER only, and so does `scripts/seed_vortex_persona_actors.sh` (header: "TASK_WORKER is implicit for every persona"). The process audit went by the roster and expected step 1 of the process scenarios to be refused. Which is intended: (a) record PROCESS_OPERATOR for the three, and rule on karl's InstancesCancel and InstancesAdvanceTimer; (b) keep the roster, remove the role on QA and name an operator persona; (c) a narrower starter permission (a product change)?

Who holds it: people who run live processes day to day"); TASK_WORKER ("Must not: start or cancel instances"); PROCESS_DESIGNER ("Must not: cancel instances"); the permission table (InstancesStart: PROCESS_DESIGNER, PROCESS_OPERATOR, TENANT_ADMIN, platform admin; InstancesCancel and InstancesAdvanceTimer: PROCESS_OPERATOR, TENANT_ADMIN, platform admin).

Karl is the `quality_manager`: he never performs step 1 of a process scenario, and no scenario has him cancel an instance or advance a timer (cancel and timer advance appear only as the UAT-RUNNER's own cleanup and timer helper in `refusal-production-order-release.yaml` and the `cleanup:` blocks).

## Decision (UAT only)

**Option (a) for anna and nina. Karl is not given PROCESS_OPERATOR (an exception to (a) as written in the issue).**

Reasons:

1. The scenarios have anna and nina raise the matter themselves: step 1 of all nine process scenarios is theirs ("logs a quality deviation", the planner's production order). A step 1 that the roster expects to be refused would make the happy-path scenarios meaningless; the process audit's expectation came from the roster, not from the scenarios. The business role of a planner or quality engineer who opens a case includes starting it, and the roster is the part that is wrong. Option (b) would force a new operator persona to start every case on behalf of the people who own it; that invents an actor the scenarios and the org structure do not have, which the BA rules forbid without the owner's decision.
2. PROCESS_OPERATOR is the only built-in role in `docs/roles.md` that lets a non-designer, non-admin person start instances. PROCESS_DESIGNER would also start instances but may change definitions, which is wider. So for UAT the least built-in role that lets anna and nina do step 1 is PROCESS_OPERATOR.
3. Karl needs nothing beyond what he does in the scenarios: he works tasks as the quality manager (routing role `role-quality-manager`, TASK_WORKER) and, in `refusal-data-and-people-access`, is expected to be refused a cost figure. No scenario has him start, cancel or advance a timer. Giving karl InstancesCancel and InstancesAdvanceTimer "on deviations he classifies himself" is not traceable to any source: `docs/roles.md` says the role is for people who run live processes day to day, and a quality manager who classifies a deviation is the one whose decision should not be undone by his own cancellation. The ruling on the issue's sub-question is therefore: **no, karl does not hold InstancesCancel or InstancesAdvanceTimer in UAT.** If the business later wants a quality manager to cancel his own deviations, that is a process-owner decision for production, not a UAT assumption.
4. Option (c) (a narrower "starter" permission) is a product change and not a UAT decision. It is recorded as the better long-term fit, since PROCESS_OPERATOR also brings DlqOperate and AuditRead that a planner or quality engineer does not need (`docs/roles.md`), but it is not required to run UAT. UAT assumption, to be confirmed by the process owner: planners and quality engineers should start their own cases.

Decision for UAT only; the process owner configures production.
