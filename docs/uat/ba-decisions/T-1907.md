## Question

Owner statement D-143 (PM, 2026-11-13, quoted from the task text): "the first production action is migrating the Qoimeks pilot client (Client 1) onto Letflow; it moves when the Letflow applications are ready (D-115)." What fits, what is missing, how does the data move and how is it rehearsed, and which conditions and owner items stand in the way?

## Answer

The full note is `docs/status/qoimeks-migration-readiness.md`. In short:
1. **Not ready.** The stock module may not be enabled for a customer tenant until ledger slices L6 and L7 are done (decision 0073, PILOT GATE row). Neither is on main (`Letflow.Ledger.authorize/3` accepts `:read | :reconcile` only; `lib/letflow/modules/stock/access.ex` says "not built (L6)"). No open issue builds them. That is the critical path.
2. **Data move half prepared.** The T-1616 converter and count compare exist (`docs/runbooks/qoimeks-rehearsal.md`). Still missing: a quantity-per-place compare, a path for codes (no file import), and rules for negative balances and movement history.
3. **The Qoimeks inventory is not here.** Fifteen questions for it are in section 1.3 of the note.
4. **Owner items:** production go, VPS money step, DNS token model, backup key holder and off-site target, production sign-in, Android or PWA, and the window.

## What I decided for UAT (the rehearsal on QA), with reasons

All four are UAT assumptions, to be confirmed by the process owner. They govern the QA rehearsal only. For production they are recommendations.
- **R-1 Opening balance, not history.** The rehearsal loads opening balances only. Reason: Letflow posts opening documents at posting time, with no backdating (decision 0079 D1, as quoted in `docs/runbooks/pilot-rollout.md`), and the T-1616 runbook already leaves history out.
- **R-2 Negative or zero balances are not loaded.** Each dropped row is listed in `report.txt`, and the rehearsal counts are judged with those rows removed. Reason: the ledger refuses a balance below zero (`insufficient_stock`, `lib/letflow/modules/stock/stock.ex`), and the converter drops rows ≤ 0 (`quantity_not_positive`).
- **R-3 A rehearsal passes only on equal quantities per item and place**, not only on equal record counts. Reason: the Qoimeks retirement criterion 7 asks for "balances per SKU and location identical before and after" (copied into the note, section 1.1).
- **R-4 Two clean rehearsals before any production step.** Reason: one run shows the tool works; a second run on a later copy shows the drops are stable.
