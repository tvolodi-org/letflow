# BA decision T-1823: MES market and standards scan

## Question
What do competing MES products offer for batch chemical / fertilizer plants and for electronics (names, scope, modules, deployment, rough price), and what do ISA-95 and ISA-88 require us to keep in mind?

## Decision
The answer is written in `docs/design/mes/06-market-and-standards-scan.md` (draft, options only). Main points:
- Fertilizer-specific public information is thin; electronics is mature and well documented.
- Prices are not public; published estimates conflict and are treated as order of magnitude only.
- ISA-95 (level 3 and the ERP link) and ISA-88 (recipe, equipment, procedure models) are modelling guides to follow, not certifications.
- Four questions buyers ask first: pilot line scope, ERP link, lot/serial tracing proof, price basis.

## Reasons
- The owner asked for public sources, cited and dated, with unverified facts marked. Each fact in the note carries a source id and a confidence label.
- No client names or confidential data were used.

## Sources opened
Web searches on 2026-11-12; result summaries and snippets only, pages not read in full. Full list with URLs: section 9 of the note. The standards texts were not read.

## Assumptions (UAT assumption, to be confirmed by the process owner)
- Granular bagging may be partly continuous, so ISA-88 batch fits only its batch steps.
- ISA-88 helps little for discrete electronics.
- 21 CFR Part 11 is not assumed to apply.
- Price figures are not usable for budget decisions.

Decision for UAT only; the process owner configures production.
