`docs/design-system/owner-review-checklist.md`; this note records the question and the choices made.

## Question

D-145 (owner decision, relayed by the PM on 2026-11-14) says that every owner finding must come back with a "lesson
added": a rule in a design-system doc, a checklist line, or a guard test. Which lesson exists today for each finding?
Which guards are missing?

## Decision

1. The checklist keeps the 16 items of the lab skill's first version with their numbers. It adds five items from the
   gate walks (17-21): the squeezed documents list (T-1875), the splitter trap (T-1873/T-1884), the Help data loss
   (T-1872), first-page-only exports (T-1955, open), and the missing PWA routes (T-1901).
2. A guard counts as one only if CI runs it. A Playwright spec that no CI job runs is marked "local e2e only". Of the
   e2e specs, only `stock-e2e.yml` runs any, and only the `gate-b-*` ones it names. So the list-detail, splitter,
   save, card-action and doc-list specs are browser tests that no CI job runs. Wiring them into CI is the first row
   of the missing-guards table.
3. Where no design-system doc states a rule, the checklist line itself is the rule ("This line"). Writing a doc
   section for those items is not part of this task.
4. Items 9 and 10 (four eyes, one-person companies) have a design (`docs/design/four-eyes-switch.md`), but nothing is
   built yet. Their guard is MISSING and comes with the build.

## Reasons

- D-145 asks for "lesson added" per finding. A guard that no CI job runs does not stop a finding from coming back,
  so calling it a guard would hide the gap the operator needs to queue.
- Keeping the lab numbering means the design gate's verdict lines ("owner checklist: items 3, 7 fail") keep
  meaning the same items.
