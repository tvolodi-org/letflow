## Reasons

1. The task text names the focus: first-month usability, ru/kk/en texts, accessibility, keyboard, print/export, import
   errors, audit visibility, notifications, security hardening, observability, performance, user documentation and
   UAT coverage. Each of items 1-10 maps to one of those; test/UAT coverage is inside items 1, 4 and 5 (each ends with
   scenarios) rather than a separate item, because the asset and stock scenarios are merged (#4067, #4078).
2. Not repeated: friendly names, responsible person, depreciation display, asset and stock UAT, flaky tests,
   1000-line performance and tenant-isolation review (T-1518 1-8, merged #4071 #4077 #4080 #4067 #4078 #4084 #4082);
   role sidebar (#4079), PWA M1-M5, runtime-update, engine-as-product and open-vs-closed designs
   (#4060, #4076, #4075).
3. Ranking: items 1-5 are what the pilot touches in its first week and cost little; item 6 is security and goes early
   because it is a deep-review path; 7 follows because support needs it from the first incident; 8-10 are larger or
   carry a decision.
4. Small items avoid the owner's decision queue on purpose: each decision-bound part is separated so the rest can start.
