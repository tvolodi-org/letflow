# T-1966: template editor for the official forms

Task T-1966 (no issue number in the task text, so the file is named after the task). The design deliverable is
`docs/design/form-template-editor.md`; this note records the question, the decisions for UAT and the reasons.

Decision for UAT only; the process owner configures production.

## Question

The owner (via the PM, 2026-11-14, quoted in the task text): "the printed official forms are NOT correctly formatted
compared with the docx templates he has; he asks for a template editor." The task asks six things: (1) how the fixed
legal fields stay fixed while a company adjusts layout, logo, header text, fonts, extra lines; (2) the relation to the
Print templates screen (decision 0068) and to the closed print template designer; (3) how the audit statement "official
forms cannot be changed by a tenant" changes; (4) a simple first step: upload an own docx per form with placeholders,
a legal-fields check, preview, versions and rollback, storage, a permission atom, file limits, ru/kk, and which path
makes the PDF; (5) how a generated form is checked against the reference; (6) slices, effort, risks, what is not
covered.

## Decisions

1. **Skeleton and skin.** The legal skeleton of each form (annex line, form code, title, header labels, column
   headings and their order, column numbers, totals label, signature roles and parts, power of attorney line, the kk
   machine-draft notice) is platform data and cannot be changed by a company. Everything else (page, margins, fonts,
   logo, header text, extra lines, widths, borders, block positions) is the company's.
2. **First step = upload of an own docx per form.** Legal texts enter the company template only as locked
   placeholders (`{{legal.title}}`, `{{legal.col.<table>.<n>}}`, `{{legal.sig.<key>}}` ...) that the platform fills
   from its catalog in ru or kk; so one template serves both languages. The upload is refused unless every legal and
   data placeholder of the form is present, the columns are in order, no unknown placeholder is used, and the file
   passes the sanitizer. Placeholder lists per form: appendix B of the design (taken from `priv/print_forms/*.json`).
3. **PDF stays the platform layout in the first step**; only the docx follows the company template. A PDF from the
   company's docx needs an office converter in the runtime image and is a later slice for the owner (S6).
4. **Open base:** upload, check, versions, rollback, docx print. **Closed extra:** the visual designer (candidate 4 of
   `docs/design/open-core-closed-candidates.md`), saving through the same open route and check.
5. **Print templates (0068) stays** for the generic renderer; official form templates are a separate kind with their
   own permission, proposal `OfficialFormTemplatesManage` (does not exist in code yet).
6. **Decision record first:** 0068 D2 rejected both a substitution-marker language (option (b), the template-injection
   threat T10 of `docs/migration/decisions/0056-versioned-documents.md`) and uploaded files (option (c)) as templates;
   the chosen docx upload with `{{name}}` markers is both. The design answers each reason (bare names with no
   expressions, values escaped by `Docx.escape/1` and `FormPrint.clean/1`, closed key list checked at upload, sanitizer
   re-writing the package; design section 2). The docx route for official forms still needs a new decision record with
   SECURITY-REVIEWER sign-off before any code (slice S0); that record states its difference to both rejected options of
   0068 D2 and addresses 0056 T10. 0068 is not edited.
7. **Audit D7 wording** stays true until the print-integration slice (S3) lands; the screen slice (S5) appends the new sentence given
   in section 3 of the design.
8. **Versions:** rows are immutable; rollback writes a new version that copies an earlier one; prints use the
   template active at print time (official forms are rendered on demand, not stored); an out-of-date template fails
   closed, never silently falls back to the standard form.

## Reasons

* The owner's complaint is measured, not assumed: the Ministry templates for annexes 25, 26 and 41 are A4 portrait
  with 5 to 8 header tables; our Z-1, Z-2 and Inv-10 docx are A4 landscape with one table (design appendix A, made
  with the read-only script `state/tasks/T-1966/peek.py`; its output is copied into appendix A).
* Locked placeholders keep the legal wording in one reviewed catalog (`priv/print_forms/messages.json`), which is the
  rule the mapping already follows: "The Kazakh wording for printing ... is taken only from the official text, never
  from machine translation" (`docs/assets/kz-forms-mapping.md` section 0). A company typing legal texts itself would
  break that rule.
* A check against the closed key list is the same field boundary 0068 D5 uses for overrides: a template can show only
  what the form mapper already gives.
* PDF from the layout JSON is the path that is proven to print every Kazakh letter: `print_layout.ex` embeds
  `priv/fonts/DejaVuSans.ttf`; the runtime image (`deploy/Dockerfile`) installs no office suite or fonts.
* The closed-candidate list itself puts "a visual designer ... on top of today's layout format" as the closed part and
  keeps the layout editing in the open build; correct legal forms must not depend on the paid build.

## UAT assumptions, to be confirmed by the process owner

* File limits: upload at most 1 MiB; at most 10 MiB uncompressed, 200 zip entries, 100:1 ratio per entry; images PNG
  or JPEG at most 524 288 bytes each (the last number is the company logo cap `TenantLogo.max_bytes/0`; the others
  have no source).
* Refused content: `.docm`/`.dotm`/`.doc`/`.rtf`, macros, ActiveX, embedded or OLE objects, custom XML, altChunk,
  attached templates, any external relationship, field codes other than PAGE and NUMPAGES, embedded fonts, tracked
  changes.
* Every data placeholder of a form is mandatory in a template ("blank is allowed, missing is not").
* Activation needs a reason; no two-person activation in UAT (0068 Q3 is still open).
* Free text a company types into its template prints as typed in both ru and kk, at the company's responsibility.
* An out-of-date template fails closed with a plain message.
* The screen says "PDF: standard layout" next to the PDF button of a form with an own template.

## Permission claim, checked

`OfficialFormTemplatesManage` (proposal) would be tenant scope. In `lib/letflow/api/authorization.ex`:
`defp core_role_allows?(:PLATFORM_ADMIN, _permission), do: true` and
`defp core_role_allows?(:TENANT_ADMIN, permission), do: permission_scope(permission) == :tenant`. So it is held by
TENANT_ADMIN and PLATFORM_ADMIN through these two catch-alls and by no other ready-made role; a company-defined role
may be given it under decision 0088. The `docs/roles.md` row is added by the slice that adds the atom.
