# T-1834: chemical plant questionnaire (RU/EN, XLSX)

**Question.** Build the client questionnaire for chemical plant prospects, in Russian and English, as one XLSX workbook.

**Decision.** Delivered `docs/design/mes/questionnaires/chemical-questionnaire-ru-en.xlsx` (four sheets, 53 questions with equal numbering in RU and EN) with a README and the generator script. Written with the standard library only. Client-facing: no internal labels, options, recommendations, price, dates or promises.

**Reasons.**
- The 15 owner-paper questions are all covered; the client questions of the liquid and granular papers were merged in; the owner's physical-data questions (density, units, conversions, tank volumes, package sizes) were added.
- 53 questions is above the 15 of the paper, as the owner asked for an extended list. Many are short; the instruction sheet allows "do not know" and approximate answers to fit about 45 minutes. This time estimate is a UAT assumption, to be confirmed by the process owner (the owner's own pre-test).

**Sources opened.** `docs/design/mes/08-owner-decision-paper.md` section 5; `02-liquid-streams-options.md` section 7; `03-granular-batch-options.md` section 7. Glossary wording (SSCC as an 18-digit logistic unit code) follows `03-granular-batch-options.md` section 8, itself from GS1 member pages, not the GS1 specification.

**Assumptions (to be confirmed by the process owner).**
- Language quality: not reviewed by a native speaker; the owner should read before sending.
- The file was not opened in Excel or LibreOffice here; it was reopened and parsed with the standard library.
- Section assignment of the physical-data questions is the analyst's choice.

Decision for UAT only; the process owner configures production.
