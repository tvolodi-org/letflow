# Letflow on your iPhone: 15-minute test

Thank you for helping. You need: an iPhone with Safari, this link, and the login you were given.
Link: @@LINK@@

1. Open the link in Safari (not another browser). Tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen". Open Letflow from the new icon.
2. Log in with the login you were given. If you close the app you may have to log in again; that is expected.
3. Scan 3 codes: tap "Scan a code", allow the camera when asked, point it at a code. Do it 3 times. The camera only starts after you tap.
4. Record one voice note: open a stock document, tap "Speak", allow the microphone when asked, say a short phrase, tap "Stop and send".
5. Work offline once: switch on Airplane mode, save one new stock document draft. You should see "No connection. The draft is saved on this device...". Switch Airplane mode off and open the app again: the draft is sent when the app is opened (not in the background).
6. Send feedback (below).
7. If something went wrong: write down the step number and what you saw on the screen. A photo of the screen helps.
