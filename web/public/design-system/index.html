20 — Date and time

Type it or pick it, and the error names the field.

lf-date is a text field with a mask and a calendar popup. lf-time is a 24-hour field with arrow-key stepping and a list of 15-minute steps. lf-datetime puts both under one label with one error line. Typed format: DD-MM-YYYY in ru, kk and uz; YYYY-MM-DD in en (read-only views show Nov 5, 2026 in en). Weeks start on Monday. Ru, kk and uz texts are machine drafts.

Date field states: empty, filled, focus, invalid (format), invalid (no such day), disabled, read-only

Calendar open: selected day (filled, bold), today (ring and dot), days outside min and max (struck through), other-month days (faint). en, ru, kk

Under 640 px the popup becomes a bottom sheet with a scrim and a Close button (see the 390 px screenshots of the document form preview). Keys: arrows move by day and week, Home and End go to the start and end of the week, PageUp and PageDown change the month (Shift: year), Enter selects, Escape closes and returns to the field, Tab stays inside the popup.

Time field states: empty, filled, invalid, disabled, read-only, list open (15-minute steps; arrows step 1 minute, PageUp and PageDown 15)

21 — Registry

A list of master data, the record on its right.

lf-registry (class lf-reg) is for items, org units, locations, employees, counterparties, lots and codes. The list pane is a compact table with search, filters, sortable headers, a status pill, "Show retired" and a pager, or a TreeView for hierarchies. The details pane is on the right: a header card (code, title, status, actions) and sections (Overview, Related, History). View, edit and create all happen in that pane, never in a modal. Under 900 px a selected record replaces the list and a Back button returns. Full preview with every state: Registry preview (standalone copy: registry.standalone.html).

22 — Journal

23 — Resizable layout

Splitters, column handles, a collapsible side nav, and screens that fill the window.

Panes of lf-doc, lf-registry and lf-journal are separated by a splitter (lf-splitter): drag it with a mouse or a finger, or focus it and use Left/Right (1 %, Shift 5 %), Home and End; Enter collapses a collapsible pane (the list, the side nav) or resets the size; a double click resets. Table headers have column handles (lf-colhandle): drag, or focus and use the same keys; double click or Enter resets; the last column takes the rest, so a table never scrolls sideways. The side nav collapses to icons. All sizes are remembered per user and screen. Data screens use lf-content--fluid and clamp() tokens: there is no centred column at 1920, 2560 or 3440 px. Contract and rules: NOTES.md, "Resizable panes, columns and remembered layout". Live: Journal preview, Document form preview, Registry preview.

24 — Lines editor

Many lines: a grid or a form, scrolling inside its own panel.

lf-le edits the lines of a document at volume (1 to 1000). Grid: type in the table, Enter or Tab move cell to cell, Enter in the last cell adds a row, paste rows from Excel, scan a code. Line by line: one line in a form, "Save and add next", the saved lines in a compact table (the mode for phones). The panel scrolls inside itself with a sticky header and totals; rows are virtual.
